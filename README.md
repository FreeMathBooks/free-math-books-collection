## 🎓 Learning Progression Across Mathematics

Building lasting mathematical fluency works best when core tools and problem-solving connect systematically to proof-based theory, analysis, algebraic structures, probability/statistics, and applied modeling:

1. **Foundations, Algebra & Trigonometry:** Build fluency with equations, inequalities, functions, graphs, and trigonometric identities.
2. **Geometry & Topology:** Connect Euclidean and analytic geometry with manifolds, metric spaces, and topological structures.
3. **Calculus & Mathematical Analysis:** Progress from single- and multivariable calculus to rigorous limits, continuity, integration, and real/functional analysis.
4. **Linear & Abstract Algebra:** Master vector spaces, matrices, and eigenvalues alongside groups, rings, fields, and Galois/homological ideas.
5. **Number Theory, Discrete Math, Probability, Statistics & Applied Math:** Apply arithmetic/modular structures, combinatorics, stochastic/statistical reasoning, and mathematical modeling.
